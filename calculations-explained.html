Calculations Explained

How the Calculations are Made:
The calculations in this app are used to determine the distribution of scores for various strategies (Tolerate, Invest, Replace, Eliminate). Each strategy is associated with a set of questions, and each question is scored based on the answers provided.

  • Client Answer: For each question, the user selects a response from the following options:
    • Yes (full score)
    • No (no score)
    • Partial/Unsure (half the score)
  • Weight: Every question has a weight (between 1 and 5), which represents the importance of the question.
  • Client Score: The Client Score for each question is calculated based on the client’s answer and the weight of the question:
    • Yes = Full weight (e.g., weight * 1)
    • No = 0 points
    • Partial/Unsure = Half of the weight (weight / 2)
  • Total Score for Each Strategy: The total score for each strategy (Tolerate, Invest, Replace, Eliminate) is calculated by summing the Client Scores of the relevant questions.
  • Distribution: After calculating the total score for each strategy, we calculate the distribution percentage for each strategy:
    • Distribution Percentage = (Total Client Score for the Strategy / Total Weight for the Strategy) * 100
    This gives a percentage representing how strongly a particular strategy is supported by the answers to the questions.

What happens when the threshold is reached?
When the configured distribution threshold of is reached, this signafiys that you have come to a valid decision on your Applications sentiment. This sentiment will be marked as green, and should be selected as the sentiment for the application.
