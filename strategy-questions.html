Calculations Explained (Thresholds can be adjusted in Admin Settings)

Individual question scores form the foundation of the TIRE assessment. Each question's importance is weighted from 1-5, and the answer determines the final score.

Score Formula:
Question Score = Answer Value × Weight

Answer Values:
• Yes = 100% of weight
• Partial/Unsure = 50% of weight (rounded)
• No = 0% of weight
Example Calculations:
Question: "Is the system stable?" (Weight: 4)
• Yes = 4 points (4 × 100%)
• Partial = 2 points (4 × 50%)
• No = 0 points (4 × 0%)

Question: "Security compliance?" (Weight: 5)
• Yes = 5 points (5 × 100%)
• Partial = 2.5 points (5 × 50%)
• No = 0 points (5 × 0%)

Category distributions are calculated by comparing the total score achieved to the maximum possible score for that category.

Distribution Formula:
Distribution % = (Total Category Score ÷ Total Possible Score) × 100

Example:
If a category has:
• Total Score: 24 points
• Maximum Possible: 30 points
Distribution = (24 ÷ 30) × 100 = 80%
Distribution Thresholds:
• 80-100%: Strong alignment
• 60-79%: Moderate alignment
• 40-59%: Neutral alignment
• 20-39%: Weak alignment
• 0-19%: Very weak alignment

TIRE placement is determined by analyzing category distributions against the configured threshold.

Placement Rules:
1. Categories must meet or exceed the distribution threshold (default: 80%)
2. If multiple categories qualify, tiebreaker rules apply
3. If no categories meet the threshold, marked as "Below Threshold"
Example Scenario:
Distribution Threshold: 80%
Results:
• Tolerate: 85% → Qualifies
• Invest: 75% → Does not qualify
• Replace: 60% → Does not qualify
• Eliminate: 45% → Does not qualify
Outcome: TIRE Placement = Tolerate

The tiebreaker system resolves situations where multiple categories exceed the distribution threshold.

Tiebreaker Rules:
1. Categories within tiebreaker threshold (default: 3%) are considered ties
2. Initial TIRE placement is considered for tiebreaking
3. User can manually select final placement when tied
Example Tiebreaker:
Distribution Threshold: 80%
Tiebreaker Threshold: 3%
Results:
• Tolerate: 82%
• Invest: 83%
→ Difference is 1% (within 3% threshold)
→ Requires manual tiebreaker selection

Question weights reflect the relative importance and impact of different aspects of the application assessment.

Weight Scale (1-5):
5 - Critical: Direct business impact, essential functions
4 - High: Significant operational importance
3 - Medium: Moderate business impact
2 - Low: Limited business impact
1 - Minimal: Nice-to-have features
Weight Examples:
Weight 5: Security compliance, core business functions
Weight 4: Performance, user satisfaction
Weight 3: Integration capabilities, cost efficiency
Weight 2: Non-critical features, minor improvements
Weight 1: Cosmetic changes, optional enhancements
